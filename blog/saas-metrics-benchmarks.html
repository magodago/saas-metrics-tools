SaaS Metrics Benchmarks – CAC, LTV, Churn, MRR

How do your SaaS metrics compare? This article summarizes common benchmarks for CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC, churn, and MRR growth. Use our free calculators to compute your own numbers and see where you stand.

Why Benchmarks Matter

Benchmarks give you context: is your CAC high or low? Is your churn in line with similar companies? They are not targets you must hit—every business is different—but they help you set goals and communicate with investors and boards. Always pair benchmarks with your own trends: improving over time often matters more than hitting an industry average.

CAC Benchmarks

Customer Acquisition Cost varies widely by segment and channel. SMB and self-serve SaaS often report CAC in the $50–$300 range. Mid-market B2B can be $500–$1,500. Enterprise with field sales can be $2,000–$10,000+. CAC also depends on deal size: a $50/month product will have a lower CAC than a $5,000/month one. Use our CAC calculator and compare to companies in your segment (e.g. from OpenView, ChartMogul, or similar reports).

LTV and LTV:CAC

Lifetime Value is typically 3x CAC or higher in healthy SaaS. Some benchmarks suggest 3:1 to 5:1. Payback period (months to recover CAC from gross margin) is often 12–18 months for B2B SaaS. If your LTV is below 3x CAC, you are either early (and improving) or need to reduce CAC or increase LTV. Calculate both with our LTV and CAC calculators.

Churn Benchmarks

Monthly logo churn for B2B SaaS is often in the 2–5% range for SMB and 0.5–2% for enterprise. Net revenue churn (including expansion) can be negative for product-led companies with strong expansion. High churn (e.g. 8%+ monthly) is a red flag unless you are in a very specific use case. Track churn by cohort and segment to see if it improves over time. See our reducing churn guide for tactics.

MRR and Growth

MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) is the sum of recurring revenue each month. Growth rates vary by stage: early-stage might target 15–25% month-over-month (or high triple-digit year-over-year); more mature SaaS might aim for 20–40% YoY. Use our MRR calculator to track total MRR. Combine with churn and new customer revenue to understand net MRR change.

Runway

Runway is less “benchmarked” and more a rule of thumb: many investors like to see 12–18+ months of runway after a round. Use our runway calculator (cash ÷ monthly burn) and plan fundraising or cost cuts accordingly. See our runway guide for detail.

Using Benchmarks in Practice

Pick a few metrics that matter most for your stage (e.g. CAC, LTV:CAC, churn, MRR growth). Calculate them consistently with our tools. Compare to published benchmarks for your segment, but focus on your own trend: are you improving month over month? Use benchmarks to set rough targets and to tell a clear story to stakeholders.

Summary

SaaS metrics benchmarks provide context for CAC, LTV, churn, MRR, and runway. LTV:CAC of 3:1 or higher and manageable churn are common goals.
